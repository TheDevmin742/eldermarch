Where the Road Stands

A short, spoiler-free recap of everything since the mountain, where each hero stands, and what to bring to the table.

What We Did

The Mountain

Skathrix the behir, Warden of the deep tunnels, dropped everyone but Kenra. Then Lloyd arrived: an orc paladin of Aurys, hired off the Phandalin job board by Qelline Alderleaf to bring Thalasstheos home. He healed the fallen, and together you blinded the behir and talked him into "dying." Then the cliff, and Vaelthundrax, the blue dragon the Warden served. You hurt him (Lloyd's javelin drew the first true blood), his fear broke you, you rallied, and when he hurled a slab of the cliff at Kenra, Thalasstheos took it in her place and went over the edge. The platform collapsed under the rest of you.

The River

You woke scattered below. Skathrix lay dead in the bracken; Kree and Kenra took horns, teeth and hide. Lloyd knocked out a screaming Wilhelm and carried him. Wilhelm followed a trail of torn blue robe to Thalasstheos, cold under an overhang, and carried her down to the river. At the reunion he pulled the old stone tablet from her satchel and the cliff splinter from her wound. The tablet flared, crumbled to dust, and she breathed. All five alive. Broke, lost, together.

The Road and Mirewatch

Three campfires of introductions: Lloyd got the whole saga, Kenra adopted his shoulder, Kree told the behir story four times. Then Mirewatch, a town the dragon's weather half destroyed. Maren Salt let you in, barely. Kree commissioned wing-fitted claws from Master Hult, and the black sword hummed at the forge loud enough that Hult noticed. Lloyd and Kree got caught in the strong-house; a lockpick is on the step and Maren saw everything. Mother Sedge gave Kenra two dormant moon-sickles ("they'll wake for wild hands") and sold Lloyd a humming silvered shovel for his plate armor and most of the behir gold. You ended the day at Widow Brann's counter, dinner paid for with Kenra's mushrooms.

Where we pick up: dusk on your first day in Mirewatch. Next session opens that night at the Reedhouse.

Where Things Stand

  • Level. 6 for everyone. Check that your sheet is updated.
  • Gold. Most of the behir trophies are spent. Wilhelm's and Thalasstheos's Phandalin coin is still stolen. Tally what you actually have.
  • Mirewatch. A Guild outpost with a job board, a smith, a reed-witch who buys monster parts, an inn (the Reedhouse, kept by Edda), and a town that needs hands. You will be here at least one more day.
  • Party name. Still "Pending" with the Guild since Phandalin. You will need one before the capital.
